# Laravel Toronto meet-up, Apr 23/2026

Laravel Toronto meet-up, Jun 11, 2026 (Chris Lemon from Vehikl talks about AI)

- food & beverages sponsored by [Maple.ca]([https://vetster.com](https://www.getmaple.c))

##
At our April meet-up at Vetster, we enjoyed a very candid look at first-hand experiences with AI. The discussion around the table was quite lively. 

After our meet-up, I thought that maybe it would be a good time to see what the greater Laravel universe is doing with AI. Who better than to invite Vehikl to speak to us about their experiences with AI. 

Vehikl is the only Canadian Laravel Partner, and has been for about forever. Based in Waterloo.

Vehikl is a one of two Laravel Toronto founding companies. I understand that Vehikl and SoapBoxHQ founded Laravel Toronto, approx. a decade ago. The meet-ups were held at SoapBoxHQ's offices in the Spadina/Queen area. 

Chris is not doing a remote presentation. He is coming in to visit us personally. 

And you should too!

Chris is speaking at PHP Tek in May (see link below). I do not see Vehikl on the speakers list @ LaraConUS 2026, but Colin Decarlo has spoken there a few times. 

Chris organizes the Guelph PHP User Group. I think Chris took over from Colin -- we'll get the full story in June. GPUG is held at Vehikl's Waterloo office. 

Schedule:
- 6:00pm to 7:00pm: meet-and-greet
- 7:00pm to 9:00pm: presentation
